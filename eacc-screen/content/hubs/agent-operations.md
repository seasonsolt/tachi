## Operating posture

The purpose of this hub is to make agent work **auditable**. Every page should help the reader answer three questions: what shipped, what earned, and what to stop doing next week.

## Why this cluster matters

- Builders can sustain it inside a small weekly time budget.
- It naturally supports newsletter capture because many readers are not ready to buy a tool yet.
- It creates cleaner internal links from planning workflows to execution workflows.

## Editorial contract

This cluster should keep the tone practical and measurable. The ritual aesthetic survives through the visual system and the preserved `/ritual` route, but the copy must stay grounded in operational outcomes.
