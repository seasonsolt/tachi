## Why this workflow exists

Discovery work often dies in chat history. A brief-to-spec workflow converts that exploration into something reusable: target audience, constraints, route plan, monetization hooks, and verification steps.

## Minimum spec fields

- the surface being changed,
- the exact routes or templates involved,
- the monetization hooks that must stay visible,
- the proof required before marking the work complete.

## Handoff standard

A good spec should let another builder answer:

1. what route changes are in scope,
2. what must remain preserved from the old experience,
3. how success will be measured in pageviews, CTA clicks, or revenue.
